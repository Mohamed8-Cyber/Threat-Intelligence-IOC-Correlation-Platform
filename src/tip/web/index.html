Indicators, and what they are tied to.

Feeds, partner bundles and analyst reports land in one store. Each indicator keeps its sources, a triage score that falls with age, and the links it shares with others. A triage score ranks what to look at first. It is not a probability. The demo data is synthetic and uses reserved .example and .test names.
